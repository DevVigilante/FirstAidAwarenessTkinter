## 1. Empathize (10 marks)
**Who might use this?** (write your hypothesis, then check it with real people)
Examples: college students, hostel residents, parents, shopkeepers, security staff, teachers.

**Interview at least 5 people.** Suggested questions:
1. Have you ever been in a situation where someone needed first aid? What happened?
2. What did you do? What did you wish you had known?
3. Do you know what number to call in an emergency?
4. Do you keep a first-aid kit? Do you know what is in it?
5. Where do you look for first-aid information today?
6. What worries you most about helping someone?
7. Have you used a first-aid app? What did you like or dislike?
8. What would make you feel more confident?

**Short survey (15+ responses, tick-box or 1–5 scale)**
- I know what to do if someone is choking (1–5)
- I know what to do for severe bleeding (1–5)
- I know the difference between a heart attack and cardiac arrest (Yes/No)
- I have a first-aid kit at home / hostel (Yes/No)
- I would use a phone or computer app to learn first aid (Yes/No)
- Preferred language (English / Hindi / Other)

## 2. Define (8 marks)
**Point of View:** [User] needs [need] because [insight from your research].

*Hypothesis to test, not a result:* "College students need a quick way to recall what to do in common emergencies and when to call 112, because they are afraid of doing the wrong thing." Replace with what your interviews show.

**How Might We (3–5):** Examples to adapt:
- HMW help a nervous bystander decide quickly whether to call 112?
- HMW make first-aid knowledge stick without a long course?
- HMW make this useful when the person has no internet?
